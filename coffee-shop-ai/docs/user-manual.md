# User Manual - Coffee Shop AI Optimization App

## Introduction

Welcome to the Coffee Shop AI Optimization App! This application is designed to help coffee shop owners optimize their operations through AI-powered insights, forecasting, and recommendations. This manual will guide you through the features and functionality of the application.

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the application URL in your web browser
2. Click "Sign In" in the top-right corner
3. Enter your email and password
4. If you don't have an account, click "Sign Up" to create one

### Setting Up Your Profile

1. After logging in, click on "Settings" in the navigation menu
2. Fill in your coffee shop details:
   - Shop name
   - Location
   - Operating hours
3. Click "Save Changes" to update your profile

### Connecting to Clover POS

1. Go to the "Settings" page
2. Scroll to the "Clover Integration" section
3. Click "Connect to Clover"
4. You will be redirected to Clover's authorization page
5. Log in to your Clover account and authorize the application
6. After authorization, you will be redirected back to the application

## Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a real-time overview of your coffee shop's performance.

### Key Metrics

- **Today's Sales**: Total sales for the current day
- **Customer Count**: Number of customers served today
- **Average Transaction**: Average amount per transaction
- **Inventory Status**: Quick view of inventory items that need attention

### Charts and Visualizations

- **Sales Trend**: Chart showing sales over time
- **Peak Hours**: Visualization of customer traffic throughout the day
- **Top Selling Items**: Bar chart of your best-selling products

### AI Insights

The Dashboard displays AI-generated insights based on your data:
- Predicted peak hours for today
- Inventory items that need attention
- Staffing recommendations

## Scheduling

The Scheduling page helps you optimize staff schedules based on predicted customer traffic.

### Viewing the Schedule

- The schedule is displayed in a weekly calendar view
- Each staff member's shifts are color-coded
- Peak hours are highlighted to help with staffing decisions

### Managing Staff

1. Click "Add Staff" to add a new staff member
2. Fill in the staff member's details:
   - Name
   - Role
   - Hourly rate
   - Contact information
3. Click "Save" to add the staff member

### Creating Shifts

1. Click on an empty slot in the schedule
2. Select a staff member from the dropdown
3. Set the start and end times
4. Click "Save" to create the shift

### AI-Optimized Scheduling

1. Click "Generate Optimal Schedule" to have the AI create a recommended schedule
2. Review the suggested schedule
3. Click "Apply" to implement the suggestions or "Modify" to make changes

## Inventory Management

The Inventory page helps you track and optimize your inventory levels.

### Viewing Inventory

- All inventory items are displayed in a table
- Items are color-coded based on their status:
  - Red: Below reorder point
  - Yellow: Near reorder point
  - Green: Adequate stock

### Managing Inventory Items

1. Click "Add Item" to add a new inventory item
2. Fill in the item details:
   - Name
   - Category
   - Quantity
   - Par level (desired stock level)
   - Unit cost
   - Shelf life (if applicable)
3. Click "Save" to add the item

### Updating Inventory

1. Click "Edit" next to an item to update its details
2. Modify the quantity or other fields
3. Click "Save" to update the item

### AI-Optimized Inventory

1. Click "Optimize Inventory" to have the AI analyze your inventory
2. Review the suggested par levels and reorder points
3. Click "Apply" to implement the suggestions or "Modify" to make changes

## Reports & Suggestions

The Reports page provides detailed analysis and AI-generated suggestions for improving your operations.

### Sales Reports

- **Daily Sales**: Breakdown of sales by day
- **Hourly Sales**: Analysis of sales by hour
- **Product Performance**: Sales data for each product

### AI-Generated Suggestions

The system analyzes your data daily and generates actionable suggestions in four categories:
1. **Inventory**: Recommendations for stock levels, reordering, and waste reduction
2. **Staffing**: Suggestions for optimal staffing during peak hours
3. **Sales**: Insights on product performance and pricing
4. **Operations**: General operational improvements

For each suggestion:
1. Review the details and priority level
2. Click "Implement" if you've acted on the suggestion
3. Click "Dismiss" to remove it from the list

### Generating New Suggestions

1. Click "Generate New Suggestions" to run the analysis on demand
2. The system will analyze your latest data and provide fresh recommendations

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

1. **Data not syncing from Clover**
   - Check your Clover connection in Settings
   - Ensure your Clover API key is valid
   - Try disconnecting and reconnecting to Clover

2. **AI suggestions not appearing**
   - Ensure you have sufficient historical data (at least 7 days)
   - Check that your inventory and sales data is up to date
   - Try generating suggestions manually

3. **Schedule optimization not working**
   - Verify that you have staff members added
   - Ensure you have peak hour predictions
   - Check that your operating hours are set correctly

### Getting Help

If you encounter any issues not covered in this manual:
1. Check the FAQ section in the Help menu
2. Contact support through the "Help & Support" page
3. Email support at support@coffeeshopai.com
